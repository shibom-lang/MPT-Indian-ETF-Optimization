🧠 How Are These Numbers Calculated?

No jargon. Here's the simple version of what happens behind the scenes.

1

Collect 7 Years of Price Data

We download daily closing prices for all 5 ETFs from January 2019 to today — that's over 1,875 trading days of real market data from NSE via Yahoo Finance.

2

Calculate Returns & Risk

For each ETF, we compute the annual return (how much it grew per year) and volatility (how wildly the price swings). Lower volatility = smoother, less scary ride.

3

Find Which Funds Move Together

Using a correlation matrix, we check if funds move together. If two funds always go up and down at the same time, owning both is like doubling down on the same bet — no safety net.

💡 Key finding: GoldBees moves opposite to equity ETFs — making it the perfect "shock absorber" in your portfolio.
4

Run 10,000 Random Portfolios

We randomly try 10,000 different combinations of how to split money across the 5 ETFs and plot them on a chart — this is called the Efficient Frontier. The best portfolios cluster along the top edge.

5

Find the Mathematical Best

Using an algorithm called SLSQP optimization, we precisely find the one combination that gives you the highest return for every unit of risk taken. This "score" is called the Sharpe Ratio.

6

The Result: Your Optimal Split

The math says: 45% Gold, 33% Junior Nifty, 22% Nifty 50 is the best mix. This achieves a 17.22% expected annual return with controlled risk — far better than splitting equally.

🏆 Max Sharpe Ratio: 0.8626 vs Equal Weight: 0.5672

📐 What is the Sharpe Ratio? (Simple explanation)

Imagine two roads to the same destination. Road A is fast but full of potholes (high risk). Road B is slightly slower but smooth (low risk). The Sharpe Ratio measures how smooth your ride is per kilometre of speed. A higher Sharpe ratio = better bang for your risk buck.

🏦 What Are These 5 ETFs?

Understanding what you're actually investing in.

N50

NiftyBees

NIFTYBEES.NS

Tracks India's top 50 large companies like Reliance, TCS, HDFC Bank. Owning this is like owning a tiny piece of India's 50 biggest businesses.

Equity · Large Cap
N+50

JuniorBees

JUNIORBEES.NS

Tracks the next 50 companies after the Nifty 50 — fast-growing mid-to-large companies with higher growth potential. Historically outperforms the Nifty 50.

Equity · Mid-Large Cap
BNK

BankBees

BANKBEES.NS

Tracks India's top 12 banking stocks like HDFC, ICICI, SBI. Moves very similarly to the Nifty 50, which is why the optimizer often excludes it.

Equity · Banking Sector
GOLD

GoldBees

GOLDBEES.NS

Backed by physical gold stored in vaults. When stock markets fall, gold often rises — making it the best diversifier. No storage hassle, no purity worries.

Commodity · Gold
LIQ

LiquidBees

LIQUIDBEES.NS

Like a super-liquid savings account that earns overnight interest rates (~6-7% p.a.). Near-zero risk. Used to park money safely while staying invested in markets.

Fixed Income · Liquid
